Put your background files in this folder:

  background.mp4          your 16:9 video (required for the video background)
  background-mobile.mp4   9:16 cut for phones held upright
  background.webm         backup copy for browsers without MP4 support
  background-mobile.webm  backup copy of the phone cut
  poster.jpg              a still frame, shown while the video loads

Easiest way: run tools/prepare-video (double-click prepare-video.bat on Windows). It makes all of these for you.

To upload them on github.com: open this media folder, click Add file > Upload files,
drag in the five files above, then click Commit changes.
